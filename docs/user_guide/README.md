## Taking Your First Assessment

### Preparation

1. **Location**: Find an open space with good lighting
2. **Equipment**: 
   - Smartphone with camera
   - Comfortable running shoes
   - Athletic clothing
3. **Setup**: Position your phone 6-10 feet away at waist height

### Recording Your Assessment

1. **Start Assessment**:
   - Tap "Start Assessment" on the home screen
   - Choose your running surface type
   - Review recording tips

2. **Camera Setup**:
   - Position yourself in the camera view
   - Ensure your full body is visible
   - Check lighting and background

3. **Recording**:
   - Run in place or on a treadmill for 30-60 seconds
   - Maintain natural running form
   - Keep your full body in frame

4. **Upload**:
   - Review your video
   - Tap "Upload for Analysis"
   - Wait for processing to complete

### Understanding Your Results

Your assessment provides several key metrics:

#### Overall Talent Score (0-100)
- **85-100**: Excellent potential
- **70-84**: Good potential with room for improvement
- **55-69**: Average potential, focus on specific areas
- **Below 55**: Beginner level, great opportunity for growth

#### Running Metrics

1. **Cadence**: Steps per minute (ideal: 160-180)
2. **Stride Length**: Distance covered per step
3. **Ground Contact Time**: How long your foot stays on the ground
4. **Vertical Oscillation**: Up and down movement while running
5. **Symmetry Score**: Balance between left and right sides
6. **Form Analysis**: Body lean, arm swing, foot strike pattern

#### Personalized Recommendations

- **Strengths**: What you're doing well
- **Improvement Areas**: Specific areas to work on
- **Training Advice**: Tailored recommendations
- **Exercise Suggestions**: Specific drills and workouts

## Tips for Better Assessments

### Camera Setup
- **Distance**: 6-10 feet from camera
- **Height**: Camera at waist level
- **Angle**: Slight side angle works best
- **Lighting**: Good natural or artificial light
- **Background**: Plain, contrasting background

### Running Form
- **Natural**: Run as you normally would
- **Consistent**: Maintain steady pace throughout
- **Relaxed**: Don't overthink your form during recording
- **Duration**: 30-60 seconds of continuous running

### Common Issues
- **Poor lighting**: Move to better lit area
- **Body not in frame**: Adjust camera distance
- **Shaky video**: Use tripod or stable surface
- **Background clutter**: Choose simpler background

## Improving Your Scores

### Focus Areas by Metric

**Low Cadence (< 160 spm)**:
- Practice quick feet drills
- Use a metronome app
- Focus on shorter, quicker steps

**High Ground Contact Time**:
- Work on calf raises
- Practice landing on forefoot
- Improve running efficiency

**Poor Symmetry**:
- Single-leg strengthening
- Balance training
- Address any muscle imbalances

**High Vertical Oscillation**:
- Core strengthening
- Focus on forward (not up) movement
- Improve running efficiency

### Training Recommendations

**Beginners**:
- Focus on consistency
- Build base fitness gradually
- Work on basic running form

**Intermediate**:
- Add speed work
- Focus on efficiency
- Target specific weaknesses

**Advanced**:
- Fine-tune biomechanics
- Race-specific training
- Performance optimization

## Troubleshooting

### App Issues

**App won't start**:
- Check internet connection
- Update to latest version
- Restart your device

**Video won't upload**:
- Check WiFi/cellular connection
- Ensure video is under 100MB
- Try again after few minutes

**Assessment stuck processing**:
- Wait up to 5 minutes for processing
- Check server status in app
- Contact support if issue persists

### Assessment Quality

**Low quality score**:
- Improve lighting conditions
- Ensure full body is visible
- Reduce background distractions
- Check camera stability

**Inconsistent results**:
- Maintain consistent running form
- Use same setup conditions
- Run at comfortable pace
- Ensure adequate recording duration
